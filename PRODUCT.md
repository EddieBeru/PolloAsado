# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

A small circle of known people — the owner plus family or a partner — each with their own account and their own private data. Nobody signs up cold, so the interface can assume a little familiarity, but it still has to be legible to someone who isn't the person who built it.

Two usage contexts, both first-class. On the phone it is capture: seconds-long sessions to log an income or expense right after it happens, often one-handed, sometimes with no signal. On the desktop it is review: longer sessions spent reading balances, budgets, and debt against each other. The mobile surface is judged on speed of entry; the desktop surface is judged on how much of the picture fits on one screen without turning into a wall of numbers.

## Product Purpose

PolloAsado is a personal finance tracker for tracking and visualizing income, expenses, savings, debts, and budgets. A savings target with a name and a goal amount doubles as a purchase goal, so planning for something is the same act as saving for it.

Success is not a single metric — it is four things holding at once: every transaction actually gets logged because entry is fast enough to never skip; opening the app answers "where does the money stand" without interpretation; debt shrinking and savings targets advancing are visible enough to make decisions from; and the whole thing replaces money anxiety with an honest picture.

## Positioning

Breakdowns, not just totals. Every income and expense splits into real relational line items, so reports answer where the money actually went — not just how much left.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, personal. It should read as your own notebook rather than as a bank: low-pressure, human, a little affectionate. Notion and Things are the closest references — roomy spacing, friendly typography, low visual pressure, nothing shouting for attention.

UI copy is Spanish, concise and utility-focused. No cheerful nagging, no robotic filler, no labels that explain the obvious.

**Direction change:** the project previously specified a minimalist dark-first flat aesthetic. That has been superseded by the warm, roomy direction above; `pollo_asado_skill.md` is updated to match. Flat color and the ban on gradients, glowing highlights, and neon still stand.

## Anti-references

- **Bank and fintech corporate.** Navy-and-gold trust theater, stock photography, enterprise dashboard chrome.
- **Spreadsheet dump.** Walls of undifferentiated rows and numbers with no hierarchy. The breakdowns are the point, so they have to stay readable rather than becoming a grid.
- **Generic AI SaaS template.** Identical card grids, uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, hero-metric blocks.
- Carried over from the project skill file: gradients, glowing highlights, neon effects.

## Design Principles

- **Warmth comes from space and words, not decoration.** Generous spacing, soft typography, and human Spanish copy carry the personality. Not gradients, not illustration, not color tricks.
- **The number is the interface.** Amounts and their breakdowns are the primary visual material. Chrome recedes so the money reads first.
- **Capture in seconds, review at leisure.** Every entry flow is judged by how fast it closes on a phone; every review surface by how much of the picture holds together on one screen.
- **Honest over reassuring.** Show the real state of the money, including the uncomfortable parts. No softening, no gamified encouragement.
- **Detail without density collapse.** Line-item breakdowns are the product's reason to exist, so depth has to stay legible instead of degrading into a spreadsheet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Contrast and legible text matter; formal WCAG conformance is not a goal for this project. Body text should stay comfortably readable against its surface, and interactive targets should stay large enough to hit one-handed on a phone.
